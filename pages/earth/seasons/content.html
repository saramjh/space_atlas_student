Why do we have seasons?

Earth is actually a little closer to the Sun during Northern Hemisphere winter, not farther. Seasons come from Earth's tilt, not its distance. Drag through a full orbit below to see why.

What's simplified

What this model leaves out.

01

Orbit shape

Earth's orbit is drawn as a circle here. It's actually a very slight ellipse — Earth is closest to the Sun in January, during Northern winter, which is the opposite of what "distance causes seasons" would predict.

02

23.5°, exaggerated

Earth's real axial tilt is 23.5° — this model exaggerates it somewhat so the effect is visible at this scale.
