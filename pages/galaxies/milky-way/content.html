What's simplified

The map is fuzzier than it looks.

01

We can't take a photo of it

We're inside the Milky Way's disc, so its overall spiral shape is mapped indirectly — through star positions, motions, and radio surveys — not photographed from outside.

02

Arm boundaries are fuzzy

Spiral arms are regions of higher star density, not walls — where one arm ends and another begins is not a sharp line.
