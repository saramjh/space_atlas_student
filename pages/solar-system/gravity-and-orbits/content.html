Why don't planets fall into the Sun?

They are falling — they're just moving sideways fast enough to keep missing. This is the same thought experiment Isaac Newton used: fire a cannonball from a very tall mountain and see what happens at different speeds.

What's simplified

What this sandbox leaves out.

01

One body, not many

Real orbits are affected by every other mass nearby — the Moon, other planets, Jupiter's pull. This sandbox has just one central mass.

02

No air resistance

Newton's original thought experiment imagined firing above the atmosphere for exactly this reason — air drag would slow a real cannonball long before it could orbit.
