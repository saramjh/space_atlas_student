What is an astronomical unit?

An astronomical unit (au) is a defined unit of length: exactly 149,597,870,700 metres (about 150 million km), close to the typical distance between Earth and the Sun. It's a convenient ruler for solar system distances, but hard to picture. Pick a real-world step size and see where every planet would actually be.
