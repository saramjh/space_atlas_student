How it works

Same elapsed time, different length of a year.

The calculator changes the unit used to count your age; it does not change how much time has actually passed.

Formula

Earth age × 365.25 ÷ planet year

First convert your Earth age to Earth days, then divide by that planet's orbital period in days. A shorter orbit produces more planet-years in the same elapsed time.

Example

12 Earth years ≈ 6.4 Mars years

Mars takes about 687 Earth days to orbit the Sun, so a 12-year-old on Earth has lived through only about 6.4 Martian years.

Meaning

Your biology does not change

This is a calendar comparison, not a claim that people age faster or slower on different planets. The same elapsed time is being counted with different orbital clocks.
