What's simplified

Why isn't this just "bigger planet, more gravity"?

01

Density matters too

Saturn is bigger than Earth but less dense than water — its surface gravity (1.07×) is barely more than Earth's, while much smaller Mercury and much larger Neptune land close together too.

02

"Surface" is fuzzy on gas giants

Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune have no solid surface — these numbers use the gravity at the visible cloud tops.
