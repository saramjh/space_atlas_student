How to read these notes

Space Atlas is an evolving educational reference. Some diagrams are scale-accurate, while others are deliberately simplified so their relationships can be seen. Each lesson states what is observed, estimated, reconstructed or illustrated. A correction in this log does not imply that an earlier visualization was scientifically equivalent to the new one.

Historical entries are reconstructed from identifiable changes in the source history. Dates reflect documented changes, not retroactive version numbering. Automated site checks verify public pages and critical files; they are not measures of improved test scores, Google indexing or learning outcomes.
