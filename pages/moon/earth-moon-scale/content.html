The spheres' relative radii and center-to-center separation use one physical scale. Perspective, camera route, travel duration, lighting and Moon surface styling are illustrative. The 12.5-second playback does not represent a spacecraft speed, the light-travel time or today's lunar phase. Earth uses a processed NASA Blue Marble composite, not live weather. The Moon surface uses a compact map from the NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit, based on Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter camera data with polar supplemental imagery (visualization by Ernie Wright / NASA SVS); it is processed for educational rendering, not a live lunar photograph. A procedural appearance is used only if the lunar map cannot load.
