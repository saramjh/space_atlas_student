Moon · Question

Why do we always see the same side of the Moon?

The Moon does rotate — it's not frozen in place. It just rotates exactly once per orbit, so the same face always points at Earth. Watch the red marker on the Moon's surface below to see the difference.

What's simplified

Why did this happen in the first place?

01

It wasn't always this way

Earth's gravity gradually slowed the Moon's rotation over hundreds of millions of years (tidal friction) until it locked into this 1:1 match.

02

Not unique to the Moon

Tidal locking happens throughout the solar system — many moons of Jupiter and Saturn are locked to their planets the same way.
