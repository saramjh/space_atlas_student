# Space Atlas: Wave 3 visualization quality and release record

Date: 2026-10-28. This is technical/browser test evidence, not a learner-outcome study.

## Five newly implemented improvements

1. TDE-007 /moon/tides/: Existing 2D spring/neap contour and NOAA-based lunar-to-solar tide-raising ratio (2:1) remain. Three bars now distinguish constant lunar contribution, constant solar contribution and a combined quadrupole resultant. The source-tested strength uses the magnitude of 6 plus 12 times the complex exponential of twice the Moon-Sun angle, divided by 18. 0 degrees: 100% of spring strength with 0-degree major axis; 45 degrees: about 74.5% and 31.7-degree axis; 90 degrees: 33.3% and 90-degree axis. This is not local ocean height. Browser testing exposed a global CSS class named sun that turned the solar bar into a glowing ellipse; corrected its appearance and verified on a 320-pixel screen.

2. LYR-008 /universe/light-year/: Existing eight-source log-distance ruler and physical elapsed/remaining light travel time calculations remain. Optional, explicitly user-initiated 10-second normalized journey controls the existing photon slider and both time readouts, pauses and stops after one cycle, stops in hidden tabs and disables when reduced-motion is set. Screen seconds are not real elapsed journey years or a change in light speed. Chromium at 390px showed ~20% after two seconds and completion at 100%, at which point playback stopped. The original source selection works when the source button is actually visible and clicked, verified separately for Proxima Centauri.

3. STR-009 /stars/life-cycle/: Existing two distinct six-stage pathways remain, with bounded manual Previous/Next and mass comparison. New opt-in 1.1-second-per-stage demonstrator advances stages and stops at the representative end, can pause, and disables animation under reduced motion without disabling manual stages. Its text warns the animated pace is not proportional to physical stellar lifetimes. Browser at 390px advanced the massive-star pathway from stage 1 through stage 3 to stage 6 and then stopped; mobile controls retain at least 44px hit targets.

4. SPT-010 /astronomy/electromagnetic-spectrum/: Added stored reference wavelength and a second marker on the logarithmic ruler. Selecting another wavelength calculates the exact current/reference wavelength ratio and reciprocal frequency/photon-energy ratios using c/lambda and hc/lambda. Illustrative waveform cycle count is excluded. Browser verified reference 550nm and selected 100nm gives 0.182 times wavelength and 5.50 times photon energy. Mobile comparison content fits the column.

5. GAL-011 /galaxies/types/: Added user-opt-in actual Three.js spatial point cloud: deterministic illustrative stars in four spiral arms, a thicker central bulge and a thin disk. The existing native viewpoint slider directly changes the 3D camera (0 to 80 degrees) and original 2D SVG fallback is retained. Optional finite eight-second angle sweep travels face-on to near edge-on and back, with Pause, visibility cancellation and reduced-motion disabled Play. Three.js is loaded only after explicit activation, and forced WebGL failure leaves the original 2D illustration readable. At 1280/390/320, rendered camera angles differ visibly; generated star coordinates are not observational data.

## Accessibility and SVG visibility audit

Source inventory: 16 routes contain inline SVG. Audited the local production-built site using Chromium in page iframes at width 320, 390 and 1280. At 320 and 1280, 27 visible SVGs and 68 text labels were traversed per width. Every visible SVG text label was checked for out-of-bounds clipping against the rendered SVG rectangle, overlaps exceeding 22% of the smaller label and, on the 320/1280 runs, whether an obscuring element owned its center hit target. Zero warning flags occurred in those defined tests. The 390 run measured label boundaries and overlap without the additional center-hit test and produced no flags. These checks are not comprehensive path/marker occlusion tests: the previously fixed Moon right-angle path remains guarded by its dedicated geometry and layer-order CI gate. No blanket zero-visual-defects conclusion is warranted.

Full mobile screenshot review: new tidal bars, light journey, star stepper, spectral comparison and galaxy 3D at 320px, plus galaxy face/edge at 1280 and 390. The added components fit within the viewport. The Tides document still has a 645px scrollWidth at 320px because Google AdSense injected an advertising iframe at that width; the new tidal bars measure 288px and are not the cause. No ad was hidden or cropped to pass testing.
