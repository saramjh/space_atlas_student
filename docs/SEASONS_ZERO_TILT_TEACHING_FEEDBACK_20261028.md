# Earth Seasons — feedback-driven, direct no-tilt classroom experiment

2026-10-28. Product route: /earth/seasons/. Applies to the teacher-use section, a device-free handout and that lesson's native Acknowledgments disclosure.

An astronomy educator/editor proposed testing the zero-tilt Earth hypothesis directly, with a globe-and-light demonstration. The existing 0-degree simulator control PRECEDED this suggestion (Git dd0a7698, 2026-09-26). It would be inaccurate to credit the educator with creating that toggle. The **new improvement** is a reproducible, teacher-led 12–15 minute prediction, two-position real-tilt control, zero-tilt comparison, evidence recording and explanation activity. The lesson also has a standalone two-page teacher guide / student worksheet that can be used offline without ads, trackers, script or student account.

A single lesson-specific entry in the public Acknowledgments data identifies the new improvement and its general feedback role anonymously. No public naming consent, official endorsement, published article, completed classroom trial, measurable child-learning gains or provider adoption is implied. A publication/editorial conversation is separate from the product implementation.

Science constraints: at zero tilt, the dominant opposite-hemisphere seasons attributable to solar angle/daylight duration would largely disappear. Earth's real orbital eccentricity still changes incoming solar energy together for both hemispheres and real weather could still vary. The classroom globe and fixed cool LED lamp are conceptual, distances/sizes not to scale, and children must not stare at bright light. NASA Earth Facts and NASA Milankovitch orbital-cycle pages remain the cited science basis. The depiction is side-on and not to scale.

Implementation: native closed details in the existing teacher-use section; no runtime JS or framework; standard CSS; handout copied with assets by the existing Python build; 0-tilt toggle remains unchanged; existing canonical/H1/JSON-LD, ads, privacy page, teacher experiment and sitemap retained.
