### Severity and corrective action

**P1 — source integrity:** Eclipse page labeled the tilt as the real approximately 5° but rendered 15° to magnify it. Fixed by disclosing 15° drawing exaggeration next to checkbox, in static model-limit paragraph, and in readout wording. Scientific geometry is *still* a simplified conceptual scene without actual node timing; do not present it as an eclipse predictor.

**P1 — misleading interaction:** Before the CDN Three.js module initialized, eclipse controls appeared active but ignored clicks and the readout started blank. Fixed with a meaningful pre-rendered explanation, visible initialization state, and controls disabled until successful initialization; after ready they are enabled, state becomes not busy and loading note hidden. With CDN blocked the source-text explanation remains available.

**P1 — accessible meaning:** Tidal-lock mode buttons previously changed only CSS `active`, not their aria-pressed state; no live announcement on model status. Added pressed-group semantics, true/false synchronized with both modes, role/status and aria-live. Both click directions verified.

**P2 — recurring enhancement timing:** Universe-scale initial `#zoomStage`, light-year `#lyReadout`, and planet-size early select options can be blank or stale before their JS modules initialize. After module readiness interactions worked. A future wider contract should render meaningful static fallback output and explicitly guard controls until ready, rather than treating a link/layout CI pass as sufficient.

**P2 — scroll/auto-click:** Chromium agent automation intermittently tried to auto-scroll an interactive control beneath the sticky 67px header and reported interception. Manually scrolling such that the control was visible allowed activation. Needs specific genuine mobile/manual friction study before classifying as a general blocker or touching the global sticky header.

**P2 — other deep-browser coverage:** These flows exercise multiple independent representative journeys; they are NOT exhaustive functional E2E for all 26 learning topics nor a formal WCAG/screen-reader audit.

## Moon tidal-locking 3D orbit clipping — screenshot-triggered regression (2026-10-11)

**Reported visual defect:** on desktop /moon/tidal-locking/ the lunar satellite vanished partly below the black WebGL canvas even though the page claimed to teach how a red Moon-surface marker stays pointed at Earth. This is a material loss of the core evidence in the experiment, not a harmless decorative issue.

**Root cause, reproduced in source:** `cameraPos=(0,27,5)` (length ≈27.46) but `controls.maxDistance=20`. `OrbitControls.update()` enforces the max distance even with user zoom disabled, pushing the camera to 20. With vertical FOV 44°, half-height at orbit plane is ~20·tan22°≈8.08 world units, **less than the 9 world unit lunar orbit radius**, before adding Moon size and the surface marker. At 320–390px portrait, horizontal FOV can impose an even tighter bound. A fixed desktop camera radius alone does not solve mobile.

**Remediation:** `maxDistance=100` so the camera isn't silently clamped; new lightweight, independent `assets/js/orbit-framing.js` computes distance from the actual container width, height, vertical FOV, lunar orbit radius, Moon/marker radius, view tilt and 15% safety margin. Tidal-locking observes only its own canvas shell with ResizeObserver, recomputes aspect/projection/distance and keeps camera direction fixed. Native locked/unlocked controls, lunar rotation, educational labels, textures and other 3D pages unchanged. Also removed one unmatched closing `</div>` from that lesson intro.

**Verification:** `ci/verify_orbit_framing.mjs` now runs on both GH Pages deploy and read-only site Health, computing perspective-projected outer bounds for every 2° around the orbit, with Moon and surface marker margins, on eight portrait/landscape dimensions (320×420, 390×420, 768×420, 1280×420, 1437×420, 280×500, 390×600, 1440×800). No projected body exceeds 93% of the half-frustum. Isolated real Chromium: 1280×850 at slider70° and 270° screenshot shows full moon and orbit inside the 1222×420 shell; 390×844 at270° screenshot shows full orbit and moon in 356×420 shell; 320×760 at actual 286×420 shell no overflow; locked/unlocked still updates ARIA and label, and desktop→mobile→desktop ResizeObserver relayout does not overflow. Screen captures are local QA evidence, not new public site assets. Original H1/canonical/JSON-LD and controlled AdSense are preserved.

The scientific content is a not-to-scale 3D teaching scene, not a physically scaled Earth–Moon gap; this fix solely restores reliable visibility of its intended surface marker through the full slider orbit. This bug would be missed by basic page-load or route-link checks, hence the targeted math+browser gate.
